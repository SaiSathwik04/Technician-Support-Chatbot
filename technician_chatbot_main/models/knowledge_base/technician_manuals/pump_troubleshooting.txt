Pump Troubleshooting Manual - Industrial Systems

CENTRIFUGAL PUMP PROBLEMS:

Pump Won't Prime:
- Check suction line for air leaks
- Verify pump casing is filled with liquid
- Ensure suction lift not excessive
- Check foot valve operation

Low Flow or Pressure:
- Inspect impeller for wear or damage
- Check internal wear ring clearances
- Verify discharge valve is open
- Clean suction strainer
- Check pump speed with tachometer

Cavitation Problems:
- Calculate NPSH Available vs Required
- Reduce suction lift if possible
- Increase suction pipe diameter
- Check liquid temperature and vapor pressure

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE:
Daily: Check discharge pressure, bearing temperature, listen for unusual noises
Weekly: Lubricate bearings, check vibration levels, inspect coupling
Monthly: Performance testing, alignment check, oil analysis
Quarterly: Bearing replacement if needed, comprehensive inspection
