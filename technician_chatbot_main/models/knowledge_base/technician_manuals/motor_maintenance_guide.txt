Motor Maintenance Guide - Industrial Equipment

DAILY INSPECTIONS:
- Check for unusual vibration or noise during operation
- Inspect motor housing for excessive heat (>65°C normal operating temp)
- Look for oil leaks around bearing housings
- Verify motor mounting bolts are tight
- Check cooling fan operation (if equipped)
- Inspect electrical connections for signs of arcing or overheating

WEEKLY MAINTENANCE:
- Record motor operating temperature at nameplate location
- Measure and record phase voltages (should be within 1% of each other)
- Check current balance between phases (should be within 5%)
- Inspect conduit connections and junction boxes
- Verify proper grounding continuity

MONTHLY MAINTENANCE:
- Insulation resistance testing with megohmmeter
- Vibration analysis of motor and bearings
- Lubrication of bearings per manufacturer schedule
- Check coupling alignment using dial indicators

TROUBLESHOOTING:
Motor Won't Start:
1. Check voltage at motor terminals with multimeter
2. Verify all three phases present (for 3-phase motors)
3. Check overload relay setting and condition
4. Test motor starter operation
5. Check for mechanical binding by hand rotation
6. Measure winding resistance with ohmmeter

Motor Overheating:
1. Measure actual current vs. nameplate rating
2. Check voltage balance between phases
3. Clean air passages and check fan operation
4. Check bearing temperature and noise
5. Tighten all electrical connections
