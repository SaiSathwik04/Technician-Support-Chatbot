Safety Lockout/Tagout Procedures - Complete Guide

ENERGY CONTROL PROCEDURE:

Step 1 - Preparation:
- Identify equipment and energy sources
- Review equipment-specific procedures
- Gather required locks, tags, and tools
- Notify all affected personnel

Step 2 - Shutdown:
- Follow normal operating procedures
- Allow all moving parts to stop completely
- Verify equipment is in zero-energy state

Step 3 - Isolation:
- Locate and operate energy isolating devices
- Electrical: Circuit breakers, disconnects
- Pneumatic: Air supply valves, bleed air
- Hydraulic: Isolation valves, relieve pressure
- Mechanical: Block moving parts, relieve springs

Step 4 - Lockout Application:
- Apply individual locks to each energy source
- Attach danger tags with worker information
- Use group lockout procedures for multiple workers
- Each worker applies their own lock

Step 5 - Verification:
- Test equipment controls (should not operate)
- Use meters to verify zero voltage
- Check for stored energy in capacitors, springs, pressure
- Return controls to neutral position

RESTORATION PROCEDURE:
1. Remove all tools and materials
2. Reinstall guards and safety devices
3. Ensure all personnel are clear
4. Remove locks (only by person who applied them)
5. Restore energy sources in proper sequence
6. Test equipment operation
