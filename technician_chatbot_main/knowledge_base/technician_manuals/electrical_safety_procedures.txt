Electrical Safety Procedures - LOTO Requirements

LOCKOUT/TAGOUT PROCEDURE:
1. Preparation: Identify all energy sources, notify personnel
2. Shutdown: Follow normal equipment shutdown sequence
3. Isolation: Operate all energy-isolating devices
4. Lockout: Apply individual locks and danger tags
5. Verification: Test equipment to ensure zero energy state

PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT:
- Safety glasses required at all times
- Insulated tools for electrical work
- Voltage-rated gloves appropriate for system voltage
- Arc-rated clothing for energized work
- Hard hat when overhead hazards exist

ARC FLASH PROTECTION:
Category 1: 4 cal/cm² minimum - Arc-rated shirt and pants
Category 2: 8 cal/cm² minimum - Arc-rated face shield required
Category 3: 25 cal/cm² minimum - Arc flash suit required
Category 4: 40 cal/cm² minimum - Maximum protection required

VOLTAGE TESTING:
- Always test meter on known live source first
- Use proper voltage-rated test equipment
- Test each phase to ground and phase-to-phase
- Verify meter still functional after testing
