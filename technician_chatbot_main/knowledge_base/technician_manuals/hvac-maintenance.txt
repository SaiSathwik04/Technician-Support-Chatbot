# HVAC Maintenance Manual - Industrial Systems

## SECTION 1: HVAC SYSTEM OVERVIEW
This manual covers maintenance procedures for industrial HVAC systems including air handling units, chillers, boilers, cooling towers, and associated equipment.

## SECTION 2: AIR HANDLING UNIT (AHU) MAINTENANCE

### 2.1 FILTER MAINTENANCE
**Filter Types and Applications:**
- **Pleated Filters:** MERV 8-13, general applications
- **HEPA Filters:** MERV 17-20, clean rooms and critical areas
- **Carbon Filters:** Odor and gas removal
- **Electrostatic Filters:** Permanent, washable type

**Inspection Schedule:**
- **Visual Check:** Weekly during peak season
- **Pressure Drop Measurement:** Monthly with manometer
- **Replacement:** When pressure drop exceeds manufacturer limits
- **Documentation:** Log all filter changes with dates and types

**Filter Replacement Procedure:**
1. Lock out air handling unit
2. Open access door safely
3. Remove dirty filter (wear dust mask)
4. Check filter frame for damage
5. Install new filter with airflow arrow pointing downstream
6. Ensure proper fit and seal
7. Record filter size, MERV rating, and installation date

### 2.2 COIL MAINTENANCE
**Cooling Coil Inspection:**
- Check for bent or damaged fins
- Look for signs of corrosion or leakage
- Verify proper drainage from condensate pan
- Measure entering and leaving air temperatures
- Check refrigerant pressures (if direct expansion)

**Heating Coil Inspection:**
- Hot water coils: Check for leaks, verify control valve operation
- Steam coils: Inspect steam traps, check for condensate removal
- Electric coils: Test heating elements, check electrical connections
- Gas-fired coils: Inspect heat exchanger, test safety controls

**Coil Cleaning Procedure:**
1. Shut down and lock out unit
2. Remove downstream filters to prevent contamination
3. Apply coil cleaning solution per manufacturer instructions
4. Allow dwell time for cleaning solution to work
5. Rinse thoroughly with water from leaving air side
6. Straighten bent fins with fin comb
7. Check coil for leaks before returning to service

### 2.3 FAN AND MOTOR MAINTENANCE
**Belt-Driven Fans:**
- Check belt tension: 1/64" deflection per inch of span
- Inspect belts for cracks, fraying, or glazing
- Verify proper belt alignment
- Check pulley condition and tightness
- Lubricate motor and fan bearings per schedule

**Direct-Drive Fans:**
- Check fan wheel balance and condition
- Inspect mounting hardware for tightness
- Verify proper rotation direction
- Monitor motor current and compare to nameplate
- Check vibration levels with portable analyzer

**Fan Performance Testing:**
- Measure static pressure across fan
- Calculate air flow using pitot tube traverse
- Check motor amperage and power factor
- Compare results to design specifications
- Plot on fan performance curve

### 2.4 DAMPER AND ACTUATOR MAINTENANCE
**Manual Dampers:**
- Check for proper operation and sealing
- Lubricate linkages and pivot points
- Verify position indicators are accurate
- Test emergency position operation

**Automated Dampers:**
- Test actuator response to control signals
- Check end switch operation
- Verify fail-safe position on power loss
- Calibrate actuator stroke and timing
- Inspect linkages for wear and proper adjustment

## SECTION 3: CHILLER MAINTENANCE

### 3.1 COMPRESSOR MAINTENANCE
**Daily Checks:**
- Record suction and discharge pressures
- Monitor oil pressure and temperature
- Check refrigerant sight glass for bubbles
- Verify proper superheat and subcooling
- Listen for unusual noises or vibration

**Weekly Maintenance:**
- Check oil level and condition
- Test high and low pressure cutouts
- Verify proper operation of unloading systems
- Check condenser and evaporator approach temperatures
- Inspect electrical connections for tightness

**Monthly Maintenance:**
- Oil analysis for acid number and moisture content
- Vibration analysis of compressor and motor
- Check refrigerant charge and leak test
- Inspect heat exchanger tubes for fouling
- Test safety controls and alarms

### 3.2 CONDENSER MAINTENANCE
**Air-Cooled Condensers:**
- Clean condenser coils monthly or as needed
- Check fan motor operation and current draw
- Verify proper refrigerant subcooling (8-12°F typical)
- Inspect for bent fins and straighten as needed
- Check condenser fan cycling controls

**Water-Cooled Condensers:**
- Monitor condenser water temperature rise
- Check for proper water flow rate
- Perform tube cleaning as indicated by approach temperature
- Test water treatment chemical levels
- Inspect water boxes and tube sheets for corrosion

**Cleaning Procedures:**
1. **Mechanical Cleaning:** Use brushes or tube cleaners
2. **Chemical Cleaning:** Circulate approved cleaning solutions
3. **High-Pressure Water:** For heavy fouling or deposits
4. **Freeze Protection:** Ensure proper glycol concentration

### 3.3 EVAPORATOR MAINTENANCE
**Performance Monitoring:**
- Record entering and leaving water temperatures
- Calculate heat transfer rate and compare to design
- Monitor approach temperature (typically 2-4°F)
- Check for proper chilled water flow rate
- Verify water treatment chemical levels

**Tube Cleaning:**
- Perform when approach temperature increases >1°F
- Use mechanical brushes for soft deposits
- Chemical cleaning for scale or biological growth
- Eddy current testing after cleaning to check tube integrity

### 3.4 CONTROL SYSTEM MAINTENANCE
**Electronic Controls:**
- Check control panel for error codes or alarms
- Verify proper operation of all sensors
- Test communication between controllers
- Update software and control parameters as needed
- Document all control settings and setpoints

**Pneumatic Controls:**
- Check air supply pressure (typically 20 PSI)
- Test operation of all control valves
- Calibrate pneumatic transmitters and receivers
- Inspect tubing for leaks or damage
- Clean and adjust control valves as needed

## SECTION 4: BOILER MAINTENANCE

### 4.1 DAILY BOILER OPERATIONS
**Visual Inspections:**
- Check water level in gauge glass
- Monitor steam pressure and water temperature
- Observe flame characteristics (color, shape, stability)
- Check for water or steam leaks
- Verify proper operation of feed water pump

**Operational Tests:**
- Test low water cutoff operation
- Verify proper pressure relief valve operation
- Check boiler water chemical treatment levels
- Test high pressure limit switch
- Verify flame safeguard system operation

### 4.2 WATER TREATMENT MAINTENANCE
**Boiler Water Testing (Daily):**
- pH level: 10.5-11.5 for low pressure boilers
- Alkalinity: 200-400 ppm as CaCO3
- Chloride content: <15 ppm
- Dissolved oxygen: <0.007 ppm
- Sulfite residual: 20-40 ppm

**Feed Water Testing:**
- Total dissolved solids
- Hardness (should be <1 ppm)
- Iron content (<0.3 ppm)
- Dissolved oxygen (<0.007 ppm)
- pH (8.5-9.0)

**Water Treatment Equipment:**
- Test water softener regeneration cycles
- Check chemical feed pump operation
- Monitor deaerator operation and temperature
- Inspect feed water tank for corrosion
- Verify proper blowdown procedures

### 4.3 COMBUSTION SYSTEM MAINTENANCE
**Burner Maintenance:**
- Clean and inspect burner assembly
- Check gas or oil supply pressure
- Test ignition system operation
- Verify proper air/fuel ratio settings
- Inspect flame scanner and controls

**Combustion Analysis:**
- Measure stack temperature (typically 300-500°F above steam temp)
- Check oxygen content in flue gas (2-4% for natural gas)
- Measure carbon monoxide levels (<50 ppm)
- Calculate combustion efficiency
- Adjust burner settings for optimal efficiency

## SECTION 5: COOLING TOWER MAINTENANCE

### 5.1 MECHANICAL MAINTENANCE
**Fan System:**
- Check fan blade condition and balance
- Inspect gear reducer oil level and condition
- Verify proper belt tension and alignment
- Check fan motor current and vibration
- Test variable frequency drive operation

**Water Distribution System:**
- Clean spray nozzles and distribution basins
- Check for proper water flow patterns
- Inspect distribution piping for clogs
- Verify pump operation and flow rates
- Check water level controls and makeup systems

### 5.2 WATER TREATMENT
**Weekly Water Testing:**
- pH level (6.5-8.5)
- Conductivity and cycles of concentration
- Total alkalinity and hardness
- Chloride and sulfate levels
- Biological growth indicators

**Chemical Treatment:**
- Corrosion inhibitors for metal protection
- Scale inhibitors to prevent mineral deposits
- Biocides to control biological growth
- pH adjustment chemicals as needed
- Bleed-off controls to maintain proper cycles

### 5.3 STRUCTURAL MAINTENANCE
**Fill Material:**
- Inspect for biological growth or fouling
- Check for proper water distribution over fill
- Look for damaged or missing fill sections
- Clean fill material as needed
- Replace deteriorated sections

**Tower Structure:**
- Inspect concrete or fiberglass for cracks
- Check steel components for corrosion
- Verify proper caulking and sealing
- Test structural integrity of platforms and railings
- Inspect access ladders and safety equipment

## SECTION 6: PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULES

### 6.1 DAILY TASKS (M-F)
- Record all operating temperatures and pressures
- Check and record energy consumption
- Visual inspection for leaks, noise, or unusual operation
- Check automatic control system operation
- Test emergency shutdown systems
- Monitor water treatment chemical levels

### 6.2 WEEKLY TASKS
- Lubricate all equipment per manufacturer schedules
- Check and tighten all belt tensions
- Test operation of all dampers and control valves
- Clean or replace air filters as needed
- Check refrigerant levels and leak test
- Perform water quality testing

### 6.3 MONTHLY TASKS
- Complete performance testing of all major equipment
- Check electrical connections and motor current
- Inspect heat exchangers for fouling
- Test all safety controls and alarms
- Review and analyze trending data
- Update maintenance records

### 6.4 QUARTERLY TASKS
- Precision alignment of all rotating equipment
- Comprehensive vibration analysis
- Infrared thermal imaging of electrical systems
- Oil analysis for all lubricated equipment
- Test emergency power systems
- Review and update maintenance procedures

### 6.5 ANNUAL TASKS
- Complete disassembly and inspection of critical equipment
- Hydrostatic testing of pressure vessels
- Calibration of all instrumentation
- Testing of fire and life safety systems
- Energy audit and efficiency analysis
- Review and update operational procedures

## SECTION 7: TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### 7.1 COMMON HVAC PROBLEMS

**Insufficient Cooling/Heating:**
- Check thermostat calibration and location
- Verify proper air filter condition
- Check for blocked or closed dampers
- Measure actual air flow rates
- Inspect heat exchangers for fouling
- Check refrigerant charge and pressures

**High Energy Consumption:**
- Compare actual vs. design operating conditions
- Check for simultaneous heating and cooling
- Verify proper equipment staging and controls
- Inspect for air leaks in ductwork
- Check equipment efficiency and performance
- Review operating schedules and setpoints

**Poor Indoor Air Quality:**
- Check outdoor air damper operation
- Verify proper filtration and filter condition
- Test for proper ventilation rates
- Check for contamination sources
- Verify proper humidity control
- Inspect for mold or biological growth

### 7.2 EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

**Equipment Failure:**
1. Safely shut down affected equipment
2. Isolate utilities (electrical, gas, water)
3. Assess impact on building occupants
4. Implement backup systems if available
5. Contact service technicians or vendors
6. Document failure and response actions

**Refrigerant Leak:**
1. Evacuate area if large leak
2. Shut down affected equipment
3. Ventilate area thoroughly
4. Locate and isolate leak source
5. Contact certified technician for repairs
6. Report to environmental authorities if required

**Water Leak:**
1. Shut off water supply immediately
2. Remove water to prevent damage
3. Check for electrical hazards
4. Assess structural damage potential
5. Begin drying process quickly
6. Document damage for insurance claims
